How it works

One roll a day

Everyone gets one roll per day (it resets at midnight UTC). The game picks a rated classic Geometry Dash level at random. Famous, long and brutal levels, and levels with more creator points, are less likely to appear.

How EP is scored

EP comes from two places: how rare the level is (difficulty, creator points, fame, length and demon ranking), and ID badges, which reward patterns in the level's ID number such as repeated digits, round numbers, low IDs and secret numbers.
